# Integrated Showroom walkthrough
Opened 2026-11-07. Slug: `showroom-integration`. Owner: Brandon's Claude Code thread. Related outcome: [Showroom issue #36](https://github.com/Agentic-Collaborative-Wiki-HAI/agentic-wiki/issues/36). Related spike: [Research-grounded Showroom walkthrough](showroom-research-tour.md), which belongs to Aslan's thread and is not modified by this work.

## Purpose
Aslan suggested bringing Brandon's interview-needs map into Figma so the two Showroom stories could be reconciled. The needs map was made outside Figma on 2026-11-01. It traces interview needs to Workshop flows, counts how many participants raised each need, and lists needs without a flow and design work without a need. Aslan's walkthrough tells one maintenance scenario with a research annotation per step. The two cover overlapping ground from different angles.

This spike puts both into Showroom and combines them into one proposed walkthrough in the style of Aslan's tour. The combined walkthrough is a proposal for team review. It does not replace Aslan's tour, and it does not settle which content the class presentation keeps.

## Presentation and ownership
The integrated frames use instances of Aslan's local Showroom components (narrative header, slide number, quote and summary notes) and the existing compact-button navigation. No new components were created. I08's gap cards are plain frames that reuse the deck's colour variables and type.

Aslan's S01–S07, every Workshop page, Erwin's pages and the Component Library were not edited. I07 contains a snapshot copy of an Erwin Workshop screen, labelled with its source and linked back to it. Brandon chose this because Aslan's Workshop has no screen for readers finding information: its "Read the agent's cited answer" frame (`939:15048`) has an empty chat panel. Erwin has not yet been told about the copy.

## Research handling
The Figma frames carry participant codes, participant counts and short quotations from the existing restricted research sources, as Aslan's tour does. This repository records none of them. The needs map's own link is not recorded here because it is link-shared and contains quotations.

The participant counts come from the needs map. Its codes appear to follow the same scheme as Aslan's annotations, since every matched quotation agrees, but nobody has checked them against the source records. Each flow-to-need link is the map author's reading of the frames, not the designers' stated intent.

## Limits
- The integrated walkthrough is a proposal. Aslan has not reviewed it, and the team has not decided what to keep.
- The two new quotations in I07 and I08 name their sources but do not link to them, unlike Aslan's annotations.
- The needs map's frame links were read on 2026-11-01. One linked frame (`939:13560`) no longer exists.
- The Cover does not link to the integrated walkthrough.
